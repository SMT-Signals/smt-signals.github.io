How to read

The bot enters at market right after the 4h candle closes, usually within 1–2 minutes. SL and TP3 sit on the exchange and are never moved. R = profit or loss divided by the initial risk, after fees and funding: an SL is always about −1R. "Late entry" means the trade was entered well after the close.

Not entered

"Skipped by rules": the bot's rules did not allow an entry (overheated market, max open trades, opposite of an open trade...). They are still logged so you can see the bot does not cherry-pick.

Verifiable, not editable

The log file lives in a public GitHub repo; every update is a commit. A signal that appears in a commit has existed since then.
