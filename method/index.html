Method and risk

Updated 28/11 03:30 Current rules frozen since 19/10/2026

What the bot does, what to really expect, and what to know before relying on any number on this site.

Signals

The bot scans 153 Binance futures symbols at every 4h close. It keeps only the strongest signals by price structure and momentum, and skips signals when the whole market is overheated in one direction.

Entry and exit

Market entry right after the close. SL and TP3 are placed on the exchange together, never moved, no early exits: a trade ends at one of the two.

Risk limits

Each trade risks 1R. Open trades are capped; same symbol same side stacks, the opposite side is skipped; new entries stop after a large daily loss.

No tuning to results

The current rules were frozen on 19/10/2026. Rules change only after testing on the full history, and any change is published.

What is an R

Each trade accepts a fixed loss if the SL is hit, called 1R. Results are in R so anyone can scale them to their own size: with 1R = 10 USDT, +2.5R is +25 USDT.

Risks to know first

  1. 1
    Drawdowns are normal

    The backtest has a −28.8R drawdown; the longest losing streak is 20 trades.

  2. 2
    Losing months happen

    About 25% of backtest months are negative; some stretches go weeks without profit.

  3. 3
    The backtest is in-sample

    Rules were chosen after seeing the data, before slippage and funding. Real expectation is lower.

  4. 4
    Entering after the bot costs

    The edge is in entering right after the close. A few minutes late on small coins can erase most of it.

  5. 5
    Exchange and coin risk

    Exchange outages, delistings and extreme moves can fill the SL far from its price.

  6. 6
    Leverage

    Futures are leveraged: the wrong position size can lose all capital even with a high win rate.

FAQ

Why are open trades delayed 24 hours?

So this page does not become a place to copy late entries. Entering hours after the bot usually gets a much worse price; the Telegram channel is where signals go.

Can the numbers be edited?

Every update is a commit to a public GitHub repo. Anyone can see the history of every file.

Why no money amounts?

Results are in R so they do not depend on account size; scale them to your own risk per trade.
